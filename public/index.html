2 ALL • 4,831 gray dots

Every filing-year, one dot.

This is the 64-d company embedding flattened to its first two principal components. Each point is one company's fiscal year, 2015 through 2024. No sector rainbow yet — they stay gray so you learn the shape first, not the tickers.

LOD: 4,000 while scrolling, 8,000 at idle. Canvas DPR1 so it stays crisp without eating your battery.
3 SECTORS • where they land

The model never saw a GICS label.

Colour the same dots by sector and the eleven centroids land in a narrow band: they span about 0.23 of the map's width, while a single sector's own spread is 0.14 to 0.19. Sectors overlap far more than they separate. That is the honest read, and it matches the recorded cluster purity of 0.486 — the model groups companies by how their financials behave, and only partly recovers the sector they were filed under.

Centroids are computed in the browser from the rows on screen, not stored.
4 INDUSTRIALS

The largest sector, and a wide one.

768 filing-years, more than any other sector here. Its centroid sits just right of the map's middle, and its spread is among the broadest — industrials do not behave like one thing.

These three steps are the three sectors with the most filing-years. Nothing else picked them.
5 FINANCIALS

The one corner the model does separate.

740 filing-years, and the most displaced centroid on the map — furthest left and lowest of the eleven, next to Real Estate and Utilities. Balance-sheet-driven businesses are the clearest structure in here.

Left and low is a direction in PC1/PC2, not a ranking.
6 TECHNOLOGY

Not off on its own.

707 filing-years, and its centroid sits mid-map. Its two nearest neighbours are Communication and Industrials, not another growth sector. Whatever the model learned, "tech" is not a corner of it. 64-d to 2-d loses local distance; we don't claim tighter than truth.

Single pop orange #EB6834 stays the only filled action colour.

Zero-deps • two fetches, no libraries

The map reads assets/map_slim.json and assets/roster.json, both derived from the model's own export. Canvas only, no chart library, no CDN. If either file fails to load the page says so and draws nothing — there is no generated fallback.

Data provenance. Every dot, tile and figure on this page is read at runtime from assets/real_data.json, the output of this repo's own model equities_mtnn_v_rebuild_d64_transformer (64-d, transformer fusion, 118 features, built 2026-08-01). Source data is SEC EDGAR XBRL 10-K 2015–2024 plus DEF 14A, yfinance and 13F/Form 4: 4,831 filing-years, 500 tickers, 11 GICS sectors. Recorded evaluation for this checkpoint: recall@10 0.910, cluster purity@20 0.486, composite 0.615. Two derived files are committed alongside it — assets/map_slim.json (the same rows with the 64-d embedding and 12 skill floats dropped for page weight) and assets/roster.json — each rebuildable with the script named in its header. The only transform applied here is a view transform: PC1 and PC2 are rescaled from their observed range into the canvas box and y is flipped so positive PC2 renders upward; PC3 exists in the export and is dropped. No value on this page is generated, seeded, sampled or interpolated. The export also carries an archetype field, which is a k-means cluster index paired with a hardcoded name whose members contradict it; it is deliberately not shown. See FINDING_archetype_names_are_not_real.md.
