Factor Exposure — The Dashboard
Altman Z
Z = 1.2·WC/TA + 1.4·RE/TA + 3.3·EBIT/TA + 0.6·MVE/TL + 1.0·Sales/TA
Plain: how many bad quarters before distress? Z > 2.6 safe, 1.8-2.6 grey, <1.8 distress. We use as Balance_Health.
Piotroski F 0-9
+1 each: ROA>0, CFO>0, ΔROA>0, CFO>ROA, ΔLever<0, ΔLiquid>0, No Dilution, ΔGM>0, ΔTurn>0
Plain: quality checklist. High F = cash-backed profit, not paper profit.
Beneish M-score
M = -4.84 +0.92·DSRI+0.528·GMI+0.404·AQI+0.892·SGI+0.115·DEPI-0.172·SGAI+4.679·TATA-0.327·LVGI
Plain: manipulation flag. M > -1.78 → earnings may be managed. Maps to exploitable.
Sloan Accruals
Accrual = (NI - CFO)/TA ; High accrual = low cash conversion
Plain: injury flag = restatement risk. Low accrual = durable.
QMJ Quality-Minus-Junk
Profit + Growth + Safety + Payout
High Profitability+Cash_Conversion+Balance_Health+Shareholder_Yield minus low = QMJ.

Formula plain: surplus = Profitability% - (100 - Valuation_Discipline%). Positive = cheap for how good it is.
Construct Validity — why these aren't vibes
  • Define construct plain English: Altman Z = distress tolerance; Piotroski = cash-backed profit quality; Beneish = earnings gamesmanship; Sloan = accrual risk; QMJ = all-in quality.
  • Operationalize: Each maps to 12 skills: Profitability (ROA, GM), Balance_Health (Z), Cash_Conversion (Sloan inverse), Disclosure_Quality (Beneish inverse), etc. 154 feats → 20 towers gated → transformer 4L4H → 64-d L2.
  • Convergent: sector purity 0.7057 lift 6.32× vs random 0.1117 — similar businesses cluster.
  • Discriminant: cross-ticker purity 0.4013 — removes own ticker, still finds peer. Not memorizing.
  • Predictive: forward IC 6M 0.007 >0, triple barrier 21.9% 10% before -7% 63d. Log guard: no ticker leakage, FY median-impute causal.
  • Threats: backtest bias, sector drift 9.8° COVID 2020-21, XBRL parsing shift 2015-2024 — mitigated by isotonic calibration + sector-neutral.
